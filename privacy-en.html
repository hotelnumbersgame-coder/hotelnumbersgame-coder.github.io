Hotel Numbers · Privacy policy

Hotel Numbers is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android devices. This page explains what data it uses and why. In short: the game doesn't ask you to sign up and doesn't know your name. Only anonymous data leaves your device: a summary of your games, which helps us improve the game, and the data needed for advertising.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Leonardo Lorenzo Longo, Modena (Italy). For any question, write to hotelnumbersgame@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

  • Game progress: levels, stars, coins, restorations and settings (including the language you chose).
  • Gameplay statistics: events such as "level started" or "level won", used to balance the game. An anonymous summary of some of these events may be sent to Google Firebase (see "Usage statistics").
  • Errors: if the game runs into an error, a technical description is saved to a file.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage and is deleted if you uninstall the game. We never receive it, except for the anonymous summary described in "Usage statistics". If you have device backups turned on (iCloud or Google), it may be included in your personal backup, which we can't see. If you choose Settings › Save game log, the game creates a file with your games that you can view and share yourself: we only receive it if you decide to send it to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional video ads in exchange for rewards. To do this it uses Google AdMob, which may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data, in order to show ads, measure how well they work and prevent fraud.

  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks you once for Apple's tracking permission. If you allow it, AdMob may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you ads that suit you better; if you don't, ads are not personalised based on your activity in other apps. The game works the same either way, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
  • On Android AdMob may use the device's advertising ID and, where the law allows it, show personalised ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your phone's settings (usually Privacy › Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before this data is used for advertising, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options. If you say no, the game still works: you may just see non-personalised ads or fewer videos.
  • In the United States some state laws (for example in California) give you the right to ask that your data not be sold or shared for advertising. You can do this at any time from Settings › Privacy options, when the option is available in your state.

For more details: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To understand where the game is too easy or too hard, we use Google Firebase Analytics. The game sends an anonymous summary of your games: levels started, won, lost or abandoned (with stars and duration), use of boosters and rewards, rewarded videos watched, purchases in the in-game shop and real-money purchases (product, price and currency). Firebase adds a random code tied to the installation, the device model and system, the language, the approximate country and the game version. We don't send single moves, your name or any other data that identifies you, and Firebase does not use the device's advertising identifier.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland these statistics are sent only if, in the privacy form, you consent to storing information on your device and to measuring content. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options.
  • We only see aggregated data; Google keeps the detailed data for up to 2 months. For more details: Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn them on, notifications (energy full, prize reel, Weekly Challenges) are scheduled directly on your device. We don't use a notification server and don't keep any device identifiers. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone but is not specifically aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

We receive no data that identifies you: uninstalling the game deletes the data on your phone. For data collected by Google, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) as explained in the Google Privacy Policy. If you think your data is being handled improperly, you can write to us or complain to your country's data protection authority.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to hotelnumbersgame@gmail.com.

Changes
