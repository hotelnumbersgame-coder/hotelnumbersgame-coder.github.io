Hotel Numbers · Privacy policy

Hotel Numbers is a game for iPhone, iPad and Android devices. This page explains what data it uses and why. In short: the game doesn't ask you to sign up, doesn't know your name and doesn't send us anything about what you do. The only data that leaves your device is used for advertising.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Leonardo Lorenzo Longo, Modena (Italy). For any question, write to hotelnumbersgame@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

  • Game progress: levels, stars, coins, restorations and settings (including the language you chose).
  • Gameplay statistics: events such as "level started" or "level won", used to balance the game.
  • Errors: if the game runs into an error, a technical description is saved to a file.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage and is deleted if you uninstall the game. We never receive it. If you have device backups turned on (iCloud or Google), it may be included in your personal backup, which we can't see. If you choose Settings › Save game log, the game creates a file with your games that you can view and share yourself: we only receive it if you decide to send it to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional video ads in exchange for rewards. To do this it uses Google AdMob, which may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location based on your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data, in order to show ads, measure how well they work and prevent fraud.

  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks you once for Apple's tracking permission. If you allow it, AdMob may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) to show you ads that suit you better; if you don't, ads are not personalised based on your activity in other apps. The game works the same either way, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy › Tracking.
  • On Android AdMob may use the device's advertising ID and, where the law allows it, show personalised ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your phone's settings (usually Privacy › Ads).
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before this data is used for advertising, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options. If you say no, the game still works: you may just see non-personalised ads or fewer videos.

For more details: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you turn them on, notifications (energy full, prize reel, Weekly Challenges) are scheduled directly on your device. We don't use a notification server and don't keep any device identifiers. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone but is not specifically aimed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Since we receive no data about you, uninstalling the game deletes everything. For data collected by Google, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) as explained in the Google Privacy Policy. If you think your data is being handled improperly, you can write to us or complain to your country's data protection authority.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to hotelnumbersgame@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If the translations differ, the Italian text prevails.
